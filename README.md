# Taxi Rank Rush

Fill your taxi. Dodge the chaos. Beat the clock.

An endless minibus taxi driving game. Pick up passengers to keep your shift clock running, dodge potholes, roadblocks, cows and pushy traffic, and climb the online high scores.
