Privacy Policy for Taxi Rank Rush

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy explains what happens to your information when you play Taxi Rank Rush ("the game") on Android or iPhone.

The short version

The game doesn't need an account and never asks for your real name, email, location, contacts or photos. Your progress is saved on your phone. The only thing sent online is your high score entry: the driver name you choose, your score and a random ID for your phone, so you can appear on the shared leaderboard.

What the game saves on your phone

To keep your progress, the game stores the following in your phone's storage:

  • Coins, upgrades, vehicles and paint jobs you've bought in the garage
  • Mission, weekly mission, trophy and daily challenge progress
  • Your driver level, XP and lifetime stats (such as total passengers and kilometres)
  • Your daily reward streak
  • High scores, including the driver name you type in (this can be any nickname)
  • Settings such as music and sound volume, vibration, screen shake and your chosen difficulty

This information stays on your phone. Deleting the app deletes it.

What the game sends online

When a run ends, the game posts your best score for that map to the online leaderboard. Each entry contains:

  • The driver name you chose (any nickname, up to 12 characters)
  • Your score, distance driven, map and difficulty
  • A random ID created by the game on your phone. It isn't linked to your phone number, account or any other app. It only lets the game update your own best score instead of adding a new entry each time.

The driver name, score, distance and difficulty are shown publicly on the leaderboard to everyone who plays. The random ID is never shown.

Leaderboard entries are stored with Supabase, a database service. They are used only to show the leaderboard. They are never sold, shared with advertisers or used for anything else.

Please don't use your real full name as your driver name. Names that are offensive may be removed.

Deleting your leaderboard entries

To have your entries removed, email us with your driver name and the maps you appear on, and we'll delete them. Using "Reset all progress" in Settings also gives your phone a new random ID, so your old entries are no longer linked to you.

What the game does not do

  • It does not ask you to create an account
  • It does not collect your real name, email, location, contacts or photos
  • It does not show ads
  • It does not use analytics or tracking tools
  • It does not sell or share your information

Internet use

The game downloads its lettering (fonts) from Google Fonts when it starts, and connects to the leaderboard to post and show high scores. Like any internet request, these services may receive your device's IP address. The game works without internet: you can still play and see the high scores saved on your phone.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. It doesn't ask for any personal information. The only information that appears publicly is the nickname a player chooses.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds features such as ads, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
